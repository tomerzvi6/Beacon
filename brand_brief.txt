--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. BRAND SNAPSHOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Beacon is a Hebrew, RTL-first iOS app that gives a family one shared, calm place
to manage a loved one's cancer care — documents, medications, symptoms,
appointments, and coordination between family members.

Name meaning: A beacon is a guiding light in the dark. For families navigating a
frightening, chaotic diagnosis, Beacon is the steady light that helps them find
their way and act together.

One-liner (HE):  ביקון — האור שמלווה את המשפחה לאורך הטיפול.
One-liner (EN):  Beacon — one calm place for your family to manage care, together.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. MISSION & PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Reduce the logistical and emotional chaos around cancer care so families can
spend less energy on coordination and more on each other.

Why it matters: 30–50% of treatment non-adherence stems from logistical
disorder — not resistance to treatment. Beacon attacks the disorder.

North-star definition of success: families using Beacon every day and feeling
that it genuinely helps them manage their loved one's care.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Families of cancer patients carry a heavy medical-logistical load under intense
emotional stress:
  • Medical documents scattered across folders, emails, and WhatsApp threads.
  • No single source of truth — who took which medication, when is the next scan.
  • Coordination friction between family members, often across distance.
  • The primary caregiver and patient are exhausted and overloaded.
  • Critical information arrives in dense clinical language nobody has energy to read.

Beacon turns that scatter into one shared, organized, calm hub.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. TARGET CUSTOMER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRIMARY PERSONA — "The Primary Caregiver"
  • A family member (often an adult child or spouse) managing the patient's care.
  • Emotionally stretched, time-poor, carrying the mental load for the whole family.
  • Tech level: BASIC. If they use WhatsApp and Facebook, they can use Beacon.
    Never assume more.
  • Wants: control, clarity, to stop dropping balls, to share the load.
  • Fears: missing something important, making a mistake, being alone with it.

SECONDARY PERSONA — "The Patient"
  • The person whose care is being managed — an ACTIVE participant, not just observed.
  • Wants dignity, to stay informed, and to retain a sense of agency over their care.
  • Beacon treats the patient as the ultimate owner of their own record.

SUPPORTING PERSONA — "The Wider Family / Circle"
  • Siblings, children, close relatives — often remote — who want to help and stay
    informed without overwhelming the primary caregiver.
  • Role-based access lets them contribute at the right level.

SUPPORTING PERSONA — "The Home Caregiver" (e.g., a hired/foreign caregiver)
  • Physically with the patient, no app account, may not speak Hebrew.
  • Uses a simple kiosk-style check-in screen in their own language
    (English, Tagalog, Hindi, Malayalam, Tamil); the family gets a Hebrew summary.
  • Campaign relevance: a proof point of inclusivity and real-world fit, not a
    primary ad target.

INSTITUTIONAL CONTEXT (B2B2C, not the consumer ad target)
  • Hospitals and oncology departments (e.g., the Rambam POC) are a distribution
    and credibility channel. Keep this in mind for trust/credibility messaging.

CAMPAIGN TARGETING SUMMARY
  • Who to address in ads: the Primary Caregiver first, the Patient second.
  • Where they are: Israeli, Hebrew-speaking, on the platforms they already trust —
    Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp-shared content.
  • Mindset at point of contact: overwhelmed, searching for control and relief.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. BRAND POSITIONING STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For families managing a loved one's cancer care, who are drowning in scattered
documents and coordination stress, Beacon is a Hebrew, family-first care hub that
turns chaos into one calm, shared, organized place — unlike group chats and paper
folders, Beacon understands medical documents, tracks the care, and keeps the
whole family aligned, with the patient at the center.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. VALUE PROPOSITION & DIFFERENTIATORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Core promise: "Everything about the care, in one calm place the whole family shares."

Differentiators:
  1. UNDERSTANDS medical documents — not just storage. Upload a document and get a
     clear summary in plain Hebrew (the heart of the product).
  2. FAMILY-FIRST, with graded roles — everyone helps at the right level; the
     patient stays the owner of their own record.
  3. HEBREW & RTL-NATIVE — built for Israeli families, not a translated afterthought.
  4. CALM BY DESIGN — made for people under stress and with basic tech skills.
  5. PRIVACY-SERIOUS — sensitive data handled with care, encryption, and audit trails.
  6. INCLUSIVE — even a non-Hebrew-speaking home caregiver can contribute.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. CORE FEATURES (framed as campaign benefits)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE MEDICAL VAULT — "Understands your documents, not just stores them"
  • Upload from camera, photos, or files; or receive documents.
  • Automatic reading (OCR + AI) returns a clear Hebrew summary, a simple summary,
    a detected category, and even suggested next tasks.
  • This is the hero feature. Lead the campaign with it.

PROACTIVE CARE — "Never miss a dose or a symptom"
  • Medication tracking, dose reminders, low-stock alerts, refill prompts.
  • Quick symptom logging with severity — ready data for the next doctor visit.

THE CIRCLE OF TRUST — "Carry it together, not alone"
  • A private family support feed: updates, reactions (heart / hug), comments.
  • Post as yourself or, with permission, on behalf of the patient.

THE DASHBOARD — "Your day, at a glance"
  • Time-aware greeting, today's schedule, today's tasks, latest caregiver update.
  • Claim / release / complete tasks so the family shares the load clearly.

ACCESS & CONSENT — "The patient stays in control"
  • Graded, per-module permissions (schedule, tasks, medications, vault, feed).
  • Patient consent gates sensitive areas; access can be granted or revoked.

HOME CAREGIVER CHECK-IN — "Everyone can contribute, in their own language"
  • A simple, large-button check-in screen in the caregiver's language.
  • The family receives a Hebrew summary with an attention/alert level.

(For campaign honesty: lead with the document-understanding + family-coordination
story. Avoid over-claiming hospital data integration as live — the hospital sync
is a roadmap/POC item, not a shipped consumer feature.)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. BRAND VOICE & TONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PERSONALITY (archetype): The Caregiver, with a touch of the Guide.
Warm, steady, competent, human. The calm friend who happens to be organized and
knows their way around a hospital — never cold, never clinical, never salesy.

VOICE PRINCIPLES
  • Warm, not sentimental. Acknowledge the hard reality without dramatizing it.
  • Calm and reassuring. Lower the reader's heart rate, don't raise it.
  • Clear and plain. Short sentences. Zero medical jargon. Zero tech jargon.
  • Respectful and dignified. The patient is a person, not a "case."
  • Empowering. Speak to capability and control, not helplessness.
  • Quietly confident. We are competent and trustworthy; we don't need to shout.

TONE DO'S
  • "הכול במקום אחד, שקט ומסודר."
  • "כל המשפחה רואה את אותו הדבר."
  • "מעלים מסמך — מקבלים סיכום ברור בעברית."
  • Use "אנחנו" / "ביחד" — togetherness.
  • Speak directly and kindly to "you" (אתם / את / אתה).

TONE DON'TS
  • No fear-mongering, no scare tactics, no guilt ("if you forget, you fail them").
  • No false hope or medical promises about outcomes/cure/survival.
  • No cold corporate or hyper-technical language ("platform", "synergy", "AI-powered
    solution"). Say what it does in human words.
  • No pity or victim framing. Families are strong; we support, not rescue.
  • Never trivialize cancer or use it as a punchline / growth-hack hook.

SAMPLE LINES (HE)
  • "כשהכול מסודר במקום אחד, יש יותר מקום פשוט להיות ביחד."
  • "מעלים את המסמך. ביקון קורא אותו, ומסביר בעברית פשוטה."
  • "אתם לא צריכים לזכור הכול לבד."

11. VISUAL IDENTITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(Grounded in the app's real Theme tokens — keep ads consistent with the product.)

COLOR PALETTE
  PRIMARY / BRAND
    Deep Teal        #16475A   Primary brand color & accent. Trust, calm, depth,
                               medical-competence without coldness. Hero color.
    Deep Teal (dark) #0F2E3D   Darkest teal for depth/gradients.
    Deep Teal (mid)  #38728C   Mid teal for gradients/secondary surfaces.
    Deep Teal (light)#578F9E   Light teal accent.

  SUPPORTING / CALM
    Sage             #DCEEDD   Pale healing green — nature, calm, recovery.
    Sage Dark        #5C8A6E   Deeper sage for icons/accents/positive states.
    Soft Blue        #C5DCE0   Gentle, airy, supportive secondary.

  ACCENT / EMOTIONAL (use sparingly)
    Coral Accent     #B22D3F   Warm red — used for alerts/urgency & moments of
                               human warmth/heart. High-impact, low-frequency.
    Coral Background #FBE4E4   Soft pink wash for warm/alert backgrounds.

  NEUTRALS / SURFACES
    Background       #EFF4F4   Warm off-white app background. Airy, calm canvas.
    Card Background  #FFFFFF   Clean white cards.
    Text Primary     #1F3845   Dark slate-teal for body text (not pure black).
    Text Secondary   #6F7E84   Muted gray for secondary text.

  COLOR USAGE GUIDANCE
    • Lead with Deep Teal + warm off-white + sage. This is the calm, trustworthy core.
    • Use Coral ONLY as a sparing accent — a heart, a highlight, a moment of warmth.
      Never flood a layout with red; it reads as alarm in a medical context.
    • Avoid pure black and harsh pure white pairings; the brand is soft and warm.
    • Maintain generous whitespace — calm comes from breathing room.

TYPOGRAPHY
    • System font, ROUNDED variant (Apple SF Pro Rounded) for titles & key numbers
      — friendly, soft, approachable, never institutional.
    • Standard system font for body — clean and highly legible.
    • Weights: Bold/Semibold for titles, Regular for body.
    • Hebrew-first; ensure any custom display font renders Hebrew beautifully.
    • Reference scale: titles ~26pt bold rounded, section ~19pt, body ~15–16pt.

SHAPE & UI LANGUAGE
    • Soft, generous rounded corners (cards ~20pt, chips ~16pt, pill buttons ~24pt).
    • Soft, subtle shadows (black ~6% opacity) — gentle elevation, never harsh.
    • Roomy 8pt spacing rhythm (2 / 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 40).
    • Large, comfortable touch targets — designed for tired, basic-tech users.
    • RTL-first composition: everything flows right-to-left; mirror layouts in ads.

LAYOUT / RTL
    • All Hebrew creative is right-to-left. Logos, CTAs, and reading order mirror.
    • Keep layouts uncluttered — one clear message per frame.

IMAGERY & PHOTOGRAPHY DIRECTION
    • Real, warm, intergenerational Israeli family moments — hands, presence,
      togetherness, quiet relief. Not stock-clinical.
    • Soft natural light, warm tones that sit with the off-white/teal/sage palette.
    • Show the patient with dignity and agency — included, not pitied.
    • Avoid: hospital-horror imagery, IV drips, distress close-ups, sad-music tropes.
    • Device shots: show the calm, organized Hebrew UI as a hero — the product is
      itself a source of reassurance.

ICONOGRAPHY
    • Simple, rounded, friendly line/solid icons consistent with SF Symbols feel.
    • A lighthouse / guiding-light motif fits the name where a light touch is wanted.

12. EMOTIONAL DRIVERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Move the audience FROM → TO:
    Overwhelmed        →  In control
    Alone with it      →  Carrying it together
    Confused by docs   →  Understanding, in plain Hebrew
    Afraid of mistakes →  Confident nothing slips
    Chaos              →  Calm

The feeling we want after seeing an ad: "Oh — finally, something that gets it and
could actually make this easier. I can breathe a little."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13. SENSITIVITIES & THINGS TO AVOID (critical for this category)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • NO medical claims about treatment outcomes, survival, or cure. Beacon organizes
    and clarifies care; it does not treat or promise results.
  • NO fear, guilt, or shame as motivators. The audience is already frightened.
  • NO exploitation of grief or cancer for attention/virality.
  • NO over-claiming features that aren't live for consumers (e.g., automatic
    hospital/EMR sync is a POC/roadmap item — don't present it as shipped).
  • RESPECT privacy in creative: never imply we read or expose data carelessly.
  • Keep AI framed as "reads and explains your documents," not as a doctor or a
    decision-maker.
  • Dignity for the patient at all times — active participant, never a prop.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14. TRUST & CREDIBILITY SIGNALS (use where appropriate)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Privacy & security: Apple-first stack, encryption for sensitive data, full
    audit trail of access, account deletion / data erasure supported.
  • Compliance posture: aligns with Israeli Ministry of Health guidance; option for
    data residency in Israel.
  • Institutional credibility: hospital feasibility track (Rambam POC) — use as a
    trust signal once formally underway, not before.
  • Cost-of-entry reassurance: built for basic-tech users; nothing to "figure out."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
15. CHANNEL NOTES FOR THE CAMPAIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Primary platforms: Facebook & Instagram (where the caregiver persona lives),
    plus highly shareable WhatsApp-friendly creative (families forward to family).
  • Format priorities: short vertical video (the document → Hebrew summary "aha"),
    carousel (the four pillars), single-image (one calm promise).
  • Always Hebrew, always RTL, always one clear idea per asset.
  • CTA tone: gentle and low-pressure — "נסו את ביקון", "מתחילים בשקט",
    not "Download now!!!".
  • Landing experience must match: calm, Hebrew, reassuring — no bait-and-switch.
